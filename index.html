How the AI Voice Lab Works

Our tool, developed by Youssef, utilizes advanced Web Audio API technology to process your voice in real-time. Unlike traditional recorders, this AI Lab allows you to capture high-fidelity audio and apply mathematical frequency shifts to change the characteristics of your voice instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Is my voice data stored on your servers?
No. All recording and AI processing occur inside your browser. Youssef Lab values your privacy; we never see or hear your files.
2. Can I use the recordings for commercial projects?
Yes! You can download your transformed audio and use it for podcasts, YouTube videos, or any other personal/commercial project for free.
3. Why do I need to allow microphone access?
To capture audio, your browser requires explicit permission. We only use the microphone when you click the "Start Recording" button.
4. Which audio formats are supported?
Our tool currently exports high-quality WebM/Ogg formats, which are industry standards for web audio and compatible with most editing software.
5. Is there a recording time limit?
There is no hard limit imposed by Youssef Lab. You can record as long as your device has available memory.
