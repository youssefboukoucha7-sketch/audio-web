Why FreeAudioRecorder.com?

Unlike standard recorders, FreeAudioRecorder.com uses a specialized DSP (Digital Signal Processing) engine. Most tools simply speed up the audio, which makes you sound like a fast-talking robot. Our tool uses Pitch Shifting, which changes the frequency of your vocal cords while keeping the speed of your speech perfectly natural.
