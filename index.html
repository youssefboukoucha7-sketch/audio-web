Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is this audio recorder free to use?

Yes, perfectly free! You can record and download your audio files without any cost or hidden fees.

Do I need to install any software?

No, you do not. Our audio recorder is entirely browser-based, making it compatible with Windows, macOS, Linux, Chrome OS, and mobile devices with modern browsers.

Are my recordings private and secure?

Absolutely. We take your privacy seriously. The recording process happens entirely within your web browser using JavaScript. Your audio data is never transmitted to our servers, ensuring total confidentiality.

What audio format is used for recordings?

Most modern browsers record in the WebM or Ogg format, which provides excellent quality at a reasonable file size. These formats are widely supported for playback.

Is there a limit on recording length?

There is no artificial limit imposed by our website. However, the maximum recording length might be limited by your device's available memory (RAM) and browser storage.
